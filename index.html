A QUICK GUIDE FOR PARENTS

When and where is Cognizance? +

Event dates and the venue have not been confirmed for this preview. Only information approved by the organizing team will be published.

Where will I find my child’s results? +

Use the Results section. Approved public results will be searchable once the team supplies and verifies them. No student information has been added to this preview.

Are these Cognizance photos and videos? +

No. The gallery shows neutral reserved spaces, and the hero uses licensed sample photography. There are no Cognizance videos yet. Owned or permission-cleared media will replace the examples before launch.

Accessibility and privacy +

The page code uses no analytics, contact forms, or video embeds. The temporary hosting service may apply its own security checks. Keyboard navigation, visible focus, a reduced-motion setting, are included. The photo viewer will be enabled after approved photographs are added. The final site needs reviewed privacy information and approved media permissions, especially for children.
