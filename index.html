A QUICK GUIDE FOR PARENTS

When and where is Cognizance? +

Cognizance Arts Fest is scheduled for November 21–22, 2026 at Malathul Badriyya Campus.

Where will I find my child’s results? +

Approved results will be available in the Results section once the organizing team publishes them.

Are these Cognizance photos and videos? +

The photos are from Cognizance ’25. Videos will be added once the organizing team confirms they can be shared.

Accessibility and privacy +

The site supports keyboard navigation and reduced-motion settings. Student photos and results will only be shared after the organizing team confirms the required permissions.
